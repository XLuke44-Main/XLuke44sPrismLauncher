# Contributing to XLuke44's Prism Launcher Fork

Thank you for your interest in my **unofficial** Prism Launcher fork.

## Project Maintenance

This is an independently maintained fork, developed and maintained exclusively by me.

I maintain this project independently and intend to retain full control over its development, architecture, direction and release process.

For this reason, **I do not accept external code contributions, pull requests, patches or collaborative development.**

All development decisions, implementation work, maintenance and releases are handled by me.

## Pull Requests and Code Contributions

Please refrain from submitting pull requests or unsolicited code changes.

External pull requests may be closed without review. This is not a reflection of the quality of the submitted work or the effort involved, but rather a consequence of my decision to maintain and develop this fork independently.

I handle all implementation and maintenance myself.

## Bug Reports and Feedback

Although I do not accept external code contributions, bug reports and constructive feedback are welcome.

Reproducible issues can be reported through the available project communication channels.

Feature suggestions and ideas for improvement are also welcome, although their implementation is not guaranteed.

I retain full discretion over which reported issues, suggestions and improvements I choose to address.

## Independent Development

This fork follows its own development direction and priorities.

I am not obligated to follow upstream Prism Launcher's development schedule, architectural decisions, feature changes or release cycle.

I make development decisions based on the goals of this fork, compatibility requirements, technical considerations and my own maintenance priorities.

## Licensing

This project remains subject to its applicable open-source licences.

My decision not to accept external contributions does not restrict or otherwise affect any rights granted to users under those licences.

## Final Note

I appreciate everyone who takes an interest in this project, uses it, reports issues or shares feedback.

I intend to continue maintaining this fork independently, with its development remaining under my direction.
