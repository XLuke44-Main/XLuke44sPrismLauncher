# Contributing to XLuke44's Prism Launcher Fork

Thank you for your interest in My Unofficial Prism Launcher fork.

## Project Maintenance

This is an independently maintained fork developed and maintained exclusively by me.

I maintain this project on my own and intend to keep its development, architecture, direction, and release process under my personal control.

For that reason, **I am not accepting external code contributions, pull requests, patches, or collaborative development.**

All development decisions, implementation work, maintenance, and releases are handled by me.

## Pull Requests and Code Contributions

Please do not submit pull requests or unsolicited code changes.

External pull requests may be closed without review. This is not a reflection of the quality of your work or the effort you have invested. It is simply my preference to maintain and develop this fork independently.

I appreciate the interest, but I prefer to handle the implementation and maintenance myself.

## Bug Reports and Feedback

Although I do not accept code contributions, bug reports and constructive feedback are welcome.

If you encounter a reproducible problem, you may report it through the available project communication channels.

Feature suggestions and improvement ideas are also welcome, although I cannot guarantee that they will be implemented.

I reserve the right to independently decide which reported issues, suggestions, and improvements are appropriate for this fork.

## Independent Development

This fork follows its own development direction and priorities.

It is not obligated to follow upstream Prism Launcher's development schedule, architectural decisions, feature changes, or release cycle.

I make development decisions according to the goals of this fork, compatibility requirements, technical considerations, and my own maintenance priorities.

## Licensing

This project remains subject to its applicable open-source licenses.

My decision not to accept external contributions does not restrict the rights granted to users under those licenses.

## Final Note

I appreciate everyone who takes an interest in this project, uses it, reports issues, or shares feedback.

I simply prefer to maintain this fork independently and keep its development under my own direction.

Thank you for understanding and respecting that decision.

— **XLuke44**
